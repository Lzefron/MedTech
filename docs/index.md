# MedTrack Privacy Policy

**Effective date: November 26, 2026**

MedTrack is a personal health record organiser for Android (`com.lzefron.medtrack`). This policy covers versions 1.2.2 and 1.2.3.

## Data handled by the app

Users may enter profile details, laboratory results, medicine schedules, emergency information, notes, and report images. This information is stored locally in the app's private storage on the user's device.

## Data collection and sharing

Report reading runs entirely on the device. MedTrack does not upload report photos or health records and does not include advertising, analytics or cloud synchronization. Recognized text is saved locally as unverified transcription alongside the reviewed results and original photo. You can also choose to show an emergency QR code. Anyone who scans it can read the information encoded in it.

## Permissions

The app may use a camera application or photo picker when the user chooses a report image. This release does not request internet permission. It requests notification permission when the user enables medicine reminders. Exported PDF files and files deliberately shared by the user leave app-private storage under the user's control.

## Retention and deletion

Records remain on the device until the user deletes the corresponding report or profile, clears the app's storage, or uninstalls the app. MedTrack opts out of Android app backup. The app does not manage copies you export or share, and you can remove those copies from their destinations. Uninstalling the app or clearing its storage permanently removes its local records.

## Security

Application data uses Android app-private storage. No API key or gateway configuration is required. Users should protect access to their device. The profile chooser is not an authentication mechanism; the app does not provide a separate PIN or biometric lock.

## Children

MedTrack is intended to be operated by adults managing their own or their family's records. It is not directed to children for independent use.

## Medical notice

MedTrack organizes user-provided information. It does not provide diagnosis, treatment, or emergency medical services. Users must review scanned values against the original document and consult a qualified clinician for medical decisions.
